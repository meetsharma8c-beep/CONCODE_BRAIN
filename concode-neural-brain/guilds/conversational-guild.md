# Conversational Intelligence Guild

The Conversational Intelligence Guild is the 14th neural guild of the CONCODE Neural Brain. It is responsible for handling non-coding user turns, greetings, social chit-chat, status questions, and general natural language inquiries.

## Responsibilities

- **Greeting & Social Handling**: Responds to greetings ("hello", "hi", "how are you", "good morning") with warm, concise natural language.
- **System Status Reporting**: Answers status inquiries and identity queries dynamically without falling back to raw code blocks.
- **Voice Loop Synthesis**: Supplies verbal summaries to the hands-free voice orchestrator (`concode_engine/core/orchestrator.py`).
- **Social Intelligence Integration**: Works alongside the Sentiment Analyzer, User Memory Store, and Dynamic Prompt Builder to maintain conversational context across turns.
